# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-30

- First English edition, based on the Korean edition 1.0.1 ([skill-dashboard-maker](https://github.com/NogadaHunter/skill-dashboard-maker))
- English skill instructions, writing guide, dashboard interface and script messages
- 8 English categories: Documents & Reports, Content & Marketing, Education & Training, Books & Publishing, Planning & Analysis, Video & Design, Data & Automation, Other
- Entries are written in English by default, or in another language when the user asks; the interface stays in English
- Trigger hints now recognize English skill descriptions ("Use this skill when…", inline request lists)
- Entry field `display_name` (entries with the Korean edition's `ko_name` are still accepted)
- MIT license
